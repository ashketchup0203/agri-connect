🌱Getting started

What is Agri Connect?

Agri Connect is an online marketplace that connects Jamaican farmers with buyers, so fresh produce can go from the farm to your pot.

How do I create an account?

Click "Create an account", choose whether you are a Buyer or a Farmer, fill in your details and agree to the Terms of use and Privacy policy. Then select "Create account".

Do I need an account to browse?

You can look around the marketplace without one. You will need an account to place orders or to list produce.

🛒For buyers

How do I find produce near me?

Browse the marketplace and look for listings from farms in your parish. Each listing shows the farmer, the produce and the price in JMD.

How do I place an order?

Open a listing, choose the amount you want and follow the steps to place your order. The farmer will be notified so they can confirm it.

I have a problem with an order. What should I do?

Contact the farmer first to sort it out. If you can't resolve it, reach out to us using the details at the bottom of this page and include your order details.

💡 Tip: Include your order details and a photo (if relevant) so we can help you faster.

🧑‍🌾For farmers

Why do farmers need to be verified?

Farmers complete a quick identity and farm verification before publishing their first listing. This keeps the marketplace safe and builds trust between buyers and farmers.

How do I list my produce?

Once you are verified, open your farmer account, add your produce with a clear photo, the amount available and your price, then publish the listing.

📸 Tip: Listings with bright, clear photos in natural light are the first ones buyers click.
How do I update or remove a listing?

Go to your listings, choose the one you want to change, and edit it or mark it as unavailable. Keep your listings up to date so buyers see accurate information.

🔐Account and password

What are the password requirements?

Your password must be at least 8 characters and include one uppercase letter, one special character and one number.

I forgot my password. What now?

On the sign-in page, select "Forgot password?" and follow the steps to reset it using your email address.

Can I sign in with Google?

Yes. Select "Continue with Google" on the sign-in page to use your Google account instead of a password.

How do I delete my account?

Contact us using the details below and we will help you close your account. You can read more in our Privacy Policy.
