Your privacy matters to us. This page explains what information Agri Connect collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it short and clear.

📝SECTION 1Information we collect

When you use Agri Connect, we may collect:

  • Account details: your first and last name, email address, phone number, parish and password.
  • Farmer verification: identity and farm details that farmers provide so we can verify them before their first listing.
  • Listings and orders: the produce you list, the orders you place or receive, and messages related to them.
  • Usage and device information: basic technical details such as your browser type and pages visited, to help us keep the site working well.

⚙️SECTION 2How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and sign you in.
  • To verify farmers and keep the marketplace trustworthy.
  • To connect buyers and farmers so orders can be placed and fulfilled.
  • To contact you about your account, orders and important updates.
  • To improve Agri Connect and fix problems.
  • To meet legal requirements and prevent fraud or misuse.

👀SECTION 3Who can see your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only when needed:

  • With other users: a buyer and a farmer see the details needed to complete an order, such as name, parish and contact information. Farmer listings are public.
  • With service providers: companies that help us run the platform, such as hosting or email services, who are only allowed to use your information for that purpose.
  • When the law requires it: if we are legally required to share information with authorities.

🔑SECTION 4Signing in with Google

If you choose "Continue with Google", we receive basic details from your Google account, such as your name and email address, to create or open your Agri Connect account. We do not see your Google password.

🍪SECTION 5Cookies

We use cookies and similar tools to keep you signed in, remember your preferences and understand how the site is used. You can change your browser settings to block cookies, but some parts of Agri Connect may not work properly without them.

🛡️SECTION 6Keeping your information safe

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including storing passwords securely. No system is completely secure, so please choose a strong password and keep it private.

💡 Tip: Use at least 8 characters with an uppercase letter, a number and a special character, and don't reuse it on other sites.

⏳SECTION 7How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations and settle disputes. When it is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

🙋SECTION 8Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • 👁️Show you the personal information we hold about you.
  • ✏️Correct information that is wrong or out of date.
  • 🗑️Delete your account and personal information, where the law allows.
  • 🔕Stop sending you non-essential messages.

To make a request, contact us using the details below.

🧒SECTION 9Children

Agri Connect is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us their information, please contact us and we will remove it.

🔄SECTION 10Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "last updated" date at the top. For major changes, we will let you know on the site or by email.

💌SECTION 11Contact us

Questions about your privacy? Visit our Help centre or email us at agri-connectsupportteam@gmail.com. You can also read our Terms of Use.
